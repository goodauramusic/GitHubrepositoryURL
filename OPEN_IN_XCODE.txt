CHORDFLOW MELODY — XCODE-ONLY

1. Put this folder in Downloads.
2. Put your JUCE folder in Downloads.
3. Open JUCE/Projucer.app.
4. Open ChordFlowMelody.jucer.
5. Click "Save Project and Open in IDE".
6. Xcode opens.
7. Select the ChordFlowMelody - Standalone target and press Command-B.
8. To build the VST3 later, select the VST3 target and press Command-B.

This project targets Intel x86_64 and enables MIDI input/output.
